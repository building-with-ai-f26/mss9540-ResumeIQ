How your information is used

AI mode sends your resume, job description, and optional LinkedIn text to OpenRouter and its model provider. Remove personal details first. ResumeIQ does not save inputs on its server. Choosing Save resume stores only the current resume text and its name in this browser’s local storage. Saved resumes are not encrypted or synced; anyone with access to this browser profile can open them. Job descriptions, LinkedIn text, and original files are not saved. Delete saved resumes from the library; clearing this site’s browser data also removes them. A job-link import sends a request to the posting website, without your resume or profile text. Non-AI comparison runs locally.

PostHog records pageviews, selected clicks, and session replay. Document inputs, URLs, saved resume content and names, and generated results are blocked from replay; custom events contain no document content.
